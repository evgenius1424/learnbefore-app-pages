Privacy

Learnbefore collects no data.

  • Pages you scan or choose are read on your iPhone. Neither the images nor the recognised text ever leave the device.
  • Your words, readings and reader profile are stored on your iPhone and, if you use iCloud, in your private iCloud database so they come back on a new iPhone. Only you can see them. We have no access to them.
  • Apple Intelligence, when it's on, runs on your iPhone.
  • No accounts, ads, analytics or tracking. The app makes no network requests of its own.
  • Crash reports reach us only if you choose to share diagnostics with app developers in iOS Settings. Apple sends them anonymously.

Settings › Erase all data deletes everything from your iPhone and your iCloud.
